About Marketscape

Marketscape is a private-equity research firm. We find privately held companies that fit what a buyer is looking for, and we contact the owners directly, as researchers rather than brokers, to learn whether they would consider a conversation about the future of their business.

Most good companies are never listed for sale. Their owners are busy running them, and they hear from brokers who want a listing, not from buyers who already understand the business. We do the research first. Each owner we contact gets a short, specific note about their own company, and anyone who is interested receives a detailed dossier on their business before any call.

We are a small team by design. Our research platform does the work of a large analyst department, and our introductions come through networks of founders we know personally.
