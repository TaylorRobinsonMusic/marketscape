What you must not do

When using the Services, you must not:

  1. Break the law. Use the Services for any unlawful, fraudulent or deceptive purpose, or in breach of any applicable law, including privacy, anti-spam and telemarketing laws.
  2. Misuse contact information. Use any contact information obtained through the Services to send messages that do not comply with applicable law (such as the CAN-SPAM Act and the Telephone Consumer Protection Act), or contact any person who has opted out or asked not to be contacted.
  3. Misrepresent Marketscape. Claim to speak for Marketscape, or describe Marketscape's role, research or communications in a false or misleading way.
  4. Resell or rebuild our data. Sell, sublicense or redistribute data, reports or dossiers from the Services, or use them to build a competing database or service, except as your client agreement expressly allows.
